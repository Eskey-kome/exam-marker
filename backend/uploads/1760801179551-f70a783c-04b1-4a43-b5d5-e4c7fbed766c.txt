SAMPLE CHEMISTRY EXAMINATION (TOTAL: 20 MARKS)

Course: Chemistry
Duration: 30 minutes
Instructions: Answer all questions clearly and concisely.

SECTION A: Short Answer (10 Marks)

1. Define an acid according to the Brønsted-Lowry theory. (2 marks)
2. State one difference between a physical and a chemical change. (2 marks)
3. Write the chemical formula for calcium carbonate. (1 mark)
4. What is the atomic number of oxygen? (1 mark)
5. State two factors that affect the rate of a chemical reaction. (4 marks)

SECTION B: Structured Questions (10 Marks)

6. A compound has the molecular formula C₂H₆O.
   (a) Identify the compound. (1 mark)
   (b) State one possible use of this compound. (1 mark)

7. Calculate the molar mass of sodium hydroxide (NaOH). (2 marks)

8. Describe briefly what happens when magnesium reacts with hydrochloric acid. (2 marks)

9. The pH of a solution is 2.
   (a) Is the solution acidic or basic? (1 mark)
   (b) What color will blue litmus paper turn in this solution? (1 mark)

10. Balance the following chemical equation:
    Fe + HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂ (2 marks)
